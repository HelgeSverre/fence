# Zalgo / combining-mark stress sample

Manual-QA document for degenerate combining-mark text ("zalgo"): base characters
with many stacked marks from the `U+0300`-`U+036F` range. It exists to find where
the virtualized editor's fixed-cell metrics and the preview's per-line layout stop
coping.

**What correct looks like**

- The caret stays on the character you clicked; arrow keys move one base character
  (or one grapheme) at a time and never land visually off by a column.
- Selection highlights cover the cells they claim to cover.
- Lines may overflow their line box, but consecutive lines must not overlap so badly
  that the base text becomes unreadable.
- Scrolling, typing and find/replace stay responsive; no per-keystroke stall.
- The preview must not reflow the whole document or blow up row heights unpredictably.

> **Warning:** the later sections are deliberately visually extreme. Section 6 is
> pathological by design.
